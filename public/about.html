ZALI Industries is a custom sublimated apparel manufacturer based in Sialkot, Pakistan. We make performance, casual, and surf apparel for brands across the US, UK, EU, Australia, and the Middle East. We're a small operation by industry standards. That's the point.

The Founder

Why I started ZALI.

PLACEHOLDER · Founder Photo
Sialkot · Founder SIA · 32.49°N
Built by hand,
by people I know.Founder Portrait · Lookbook Vol. 04

I grew up around fabric. My family has been in Sialkot's apparel industry for years before I started ZALI, and I spent my early career working alongside cutters, stitchers, and printers in some of the city's largest factories. What I noticed was a pattern: the best people made the worst clients' garments, because the people doing the actual work never got to talk to the people buying the goods.

So in 2020, I built ZALI. The idea was simple: keep the team small, keep the communication direct, and let brands talk to the people who are actually making their gear. No agents. No layers. No "I'll ask the factory and get back to you."

Today we're a team of around 15. I personally manage every client account, every design review, and every QC sign-off. When you message us, you're messaging me or someone I trained. When something goes wrong, I'm the one who answers for it. When something goes right, the team here gets the credit.

"We're small enough to know every garment that leaves the building, and that's exactly the size we want to stay."

If you're looking for a faceless factory that pumps out 10,000 units a week, that's not us. If you want to work with a team that learns your brand, gets the small details right, and sends you a WhatsApp when your order ships, you're in the right place.

Zyian Ali
Founder · ZALI Industries
Why Sialkot

The world's capital of sportswear.

Most of the world's match-grade soccer balls, Olympic-spec hockey gear, and pro fightwear comes from Sialkot. The city has been making sporting goods for over a century, since the British Raj era. Three generations of stitchers, cutters, and printers have grown up in this trade.

When you order from ZALI, you're not just getting a factory. You're getting access to an entire ecosystem of suppliers, fabric mills, trim makers, and specialty artisans, all within a 15-minute drive from our facility.

What We Stand For

Three things, non-negotiable.

Everything else is up for discussion. These aren't.

01

Direct, not delegated.

Every client account is handled by the founder or a senior team member who reports directly. No agents, no layers, no "I'll check and get back." If you have a question, you get a real answer fast.

02

Built in-house.

Sublimation, cutting, stitching, finishing, QC, packing — all under one roof. Nothing outsourced. We know every machine, every operator, and every step of how your gear gets made.

03

Sample before bulk.

Always. Even if you're in a rush. Even if you've ordered 10 times before. We don't run bulk on a design you haven't approved in physical form. The cost is refunded, so it's effectively zero risk.

Talk to us

Same person from first message to last shipment.

Send us a project, a question, or just a hello. The reply comes from a real person within 24 hours, every time.
