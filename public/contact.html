What helps us most

  • A reference image (screenshot, sketch, or competitor photo)
  • Tech pack if you have one (PDF, AI, PSD all fine)
  • Approximate quantity and your target launch date
  • Any fabric, weight, or print method preferences
  • Your shipping country (affects duties and timing)
  • Sample requirement before bulk: yes (recommended) or no
  • If you have your own size chart, attach it — we'll work to your spec, not ours
Free Service

Shipping Terms

We ship globally.

We support multiple international shipping terms — DDU, DDP, CNF, and FOB — so brands, suppliers, and distributors can manage imports in whatever way works best for them. Default is DDP (duties prepaid, door-to-door) via DHL, FedEx, or UPS; sea freight available for orders over 1,000 pieces.
